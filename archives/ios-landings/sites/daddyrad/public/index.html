Know what is happening on your Mac.

DaddyRad brings storage, performance, coding agents, browsing and network insight into one workspace. See the evidence, then decide what to do.

01 · STORAGE

Find what takes space.

Explore real storage culprits, inspect cleanup candidates and review measured results before making room.

02 · PERFORMANCE

Understand the load.

Inspect processes and resource use to diagnose a slow Mac, then review a process before choosing to stop it.

03 · AGENTS & BROWSER

See your activity.

Inspect coding-agent context and browsing activity, and manage site blocking from the same app.

04 · NETWORK

Check your connection.

See your Wi-Fi link and live traffic, then run an internet test when you need one.

Start with a question.

Follow the evidence across your Mac, from a full disk to a busy process or a connection that feels slow.

What is filling my disk?
Explore the space used by projects, caches and other large paths. Inspect candidates before cleanup and see the measured result.
What is using resources?
Inspect processes and resource use. A busy process is a clue, so review it before deciding whether to stop it.
What are my agents and browser doing?
Explore coding-agent context and browser activity, and choose which sites to block.
Is it my Wi-Fi or the internet?
Inspect the Wi-Fi link and live traffic, and run an on-demand internet test for another view of the connection.

Local-first
Start with evidence on your Mac. Inspect evidence from your Mac and review the relevant privacy and permission boundaries.
Measured, not guessed
Every claim comes from a capture you can inspect. Absent evidence is shown as absent.
Review before action
DaddyRad diagnoses and surfaces candidates; stopping a process or deleting a file is always your call.
